# Knowledge-base provenance

The bundled knowledge base was authored from the user-supplied HTML text file of the SRIT homepage (`https://www.srit.ac.in/`) and the navigation URLs present in that text. The chatbot does not import the entire page source or treat all embedded social posts as current information.

## Official URLs referenced

- Homepage: https://www.srit.ac.in/
- Overview: https://www.srit.ac.in/overview/
- Courses offered: https://www.srit.ac.in/courses-offered/
- Admission procedure: https://www.srit.ac.in/admission-procedure/
- Fee structure: https://www.srit.ac.in/fee-structure/
- Scholarships: https://www.srit.ac.in/scholarships/
- Training and placement statistics: https://www.srit.ac.in/training-placement-statistics/
- Library: https://www.srit.ac.in/library/
- Transport: https://www.srit.ac.in/transport/
- Hostels: https://www.srit.ac.in/hostels/
- Contact: https://www.srit.ac.in/contact-us/

## Data handling choices

- The current fee, current cutoffs, exact eligibility rules, live vacancies, current deadlines and current hostel/transport details are intentionally not guessed.
- The homepage snapshot's 2024–25 placement counts are described as historical snapshot data.
- A few old accreditation milestones were found in an embedded social profile within the source HTML. The chatbot labels them as historical and directs users to official regulatory pages for current verification.
- The page menu lists the branches/program areas in `data/college_knowledge.json`; the bot directs users to the official courses page to confirm current intake and approved offerings.
